About PowerX Engineering

Engineering The World's Most Complex Industrial Challenges

PowerX Engineering Ltd is a premier international engineering firm headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with operations spanning Asia, the Middle East, and Africa. Since our founding in 2005, we have delivered mission-critical engineering solutions to governments, multinational corporations, and industrial conglomerates.

Our multidisciplinary team of certified engineers, project managers, and technical specialists brings together deep expertise across electrical systems, power generation, automation, civil construction, and renewable energy — ensuring every project is delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest international standards.

Our Mission

To deliver technically superior, cost-effective engineering solutions that power industrial growth and improve lives globally.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted engineering partner in emerging markets, recognized for innovation, safety, and sustainable excellence.
